Ibra, known by the handle dystopian, is a Winnipeg-based fashion designer crafting handmade garments since 2024, with a signature aesthetic rooted in a futuristic, apocalyptic world. Every piece is designed and constructed from scratch, built to feel like it belongs to a story yet to unfold.

As Fashion Director at BRICKTV, Ibra leads all costume design for the studio’s productions, shaping the visual identity of its characters and worlds through wardrobe.

In addition to this role, Ibra spearheads styling and ready-to-wear design while collaborating with photographer Loïc, translating their distinct vision into fashion tailored for the lens.
