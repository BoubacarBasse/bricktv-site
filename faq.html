Questions

Ask us anything.

What does a project cost?

Every project is priced individually, after a consultation. No fixed menus, the first call is where we scope it.

Do you record vocals and instruments?

Yes, recording, original composition, and mixing & mastering. Music lessons too.

Do you shoot weddings?

Yes, wedding films and event photography, alongside our commercial and editorial work.

Can you make clothing for a shoot or an event?

Yes, custom-made patterns and outfit consultation, for wardrobe on camera or off.

Where do you work?

Winnipeg and roughly 200 km around it. Farther, anywhere really, when the project makes sense. Like a contractor, our equipment travels with us.

Are you a marketing agency?

No, and that’s the point. We’re the artists agencies hire. You get the craft direct, without the markup. We make the work; we don’t run ad campaigns or promise anything will “blow up”.

What languages do you work in?

English, French, Arabic, Darija, and Swahili.

When can I reach you?

We’re on this brick 24/7, call or write any time and we’ll get back to you fast.
